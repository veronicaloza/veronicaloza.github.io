Veronica Loza is a multidisciplinary graphic designer based in New York whose practice focuses on brand identity, editorial design, and web interactions. Originally from Russia, she is currently a research assistant for Nika Simovich Fisher, the President of Digital Design Collective at Parsons. Her previous experience includes Oscar de la Renta, BBDO Contrapunto Russia, and a range of independent and freelance projects.
